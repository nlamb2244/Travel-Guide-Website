What to pack for a long drive

A road-tested packing framework for comfort, emergencies, and avoiding a cluttered vehicle.

Practical travel guide·Evergreen planning tips

Quick take: A road-tested packing framework for comfort, emergencies, and avoiding a cluttered vehicle.

Keep the essentials within reach

Your identification, wallet, phone, charging cable, water, medications, and a few snacks belong somewhere accessible. A well-packed car is useful only if you can find what you need without unloading half of it.

Build a small vehicle kit

Consider a flashlight, basic first-aid supplies, tire gauge, appropriate seasonal clothing, a working spare or tire repair setup, and necessary tools. Check the vehicle’s tire condition, fluid levels and service status before a long drive.

Pack for weather changes

Travel across regions can bring dramatic changes in temperature or rain. Layered clothing is often more practical than carrying multiple bulky outfits. Bring shoes you can actually walk in.

Leave space on purpose

Overpacking makes every hotel stop harder. Try a simple overnight bag for what you need at your next stop and keep seldom-used gear separate. A little empty space also makes room for things you discover along the way.

Travel note: Prices, route conditions, schedules and rules can change. Check the relevant official sources and service providers before making bookings or driving.
