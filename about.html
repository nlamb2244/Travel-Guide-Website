The story behind 48 & Beyond

Good travel advice starts with getting out there.

Less chasing trends. More time on the road and making the most of where you are.

From the road, for the road.

48 & Beyond was conceived around an unusually broad travel perspective: experience traveling through 48 U.S. states, plus visits to Canada and Mexico.

This site aims to turn that perspective into useful guides for people planning weekend escapes, work trips and longer journeys. The emphasis is practical: lodging decisions, budgets, packing, routes and memorable stops.

The articles currently on the website are starter editorial drafts. They are not presented as reviews of specific businesses or places personally visited. Destination features will be expanded and fact-checked before publication.

Our principles

Clarity over hype.

  • Never invent ratings or personal experiences.
  • Make paid relationships clear.
  • Flag facts that can change, such as prices and opening hours.
  • Help travelers make their own choices.
